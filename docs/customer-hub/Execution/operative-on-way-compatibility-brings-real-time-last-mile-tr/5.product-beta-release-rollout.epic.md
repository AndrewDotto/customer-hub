# Product Beta Release & Rollout

## Strategic Context

A controlled beta rollout allows field validation with real operatives and customers in live operating environments, providing crucial feedback on tracking fidelity and arrival notification clarity. Phased feature activation and customer cohort management minimize operational risk while gathering necessary metrics to validate general availability readiness.

## Success Criteria

* Successfully onboard and activate 2 target beta customer organizations within 2 weeks of release.
* Achieve minimum 90% tracking session completion rate across live operative field trips.
* Establish automated feedback capture and bi-weekly review cycle with participating pilot customers.

## Scope

### In Scope

* Beta tenant provisioning, configuration verification, and initial activation.
* Usage telemetry dashboards tracking operative sessions, ETA accuracy, and delivery confirmations.
* Customer feedback collection channels and beta exit criteria evaluation.

### Out of Scope

* Unrestricted public customer self-serve provisioning (deferred to General Availability).
* Integration with non-standard legacy third-party dispatch platforms outside core scope.

## Acceptance Criteria

WHEN an approved beta customer organization is provisioned, last-mile tracking capabilities exclusively for designated tenant IDs.

WHERE an operative completes a field delivery, the telemetry system SHALL location updates, and completion confirmation events.

IF tracking telemetry indicates an error rate above 2% for any active tenant, THEN the monitoring service SHALL alert the product team immediately.

WHEN the beta evaluation period concludes, the product manager SHALL evaluate collected metrics against the GA readiness criteria.

## Business Rules

* Beta activation must be gated by tenant-level feature flags with instant kill-switch capability.
* Maximum of 10 pilot customer organizations permitted during the initial Beta 1.0 phase.
* Beta participation requires signed pilot agreements outlining telemetry collection and feedback participation.

## Dependencies

* Production Deployment & Readiness epic completed and verified in production.
* GTM & Delivery Enablement epic completed with support teams primed for customer inquiries.
