# Comprehensive QA & Validation

## Strategic Context
With the core tracking and notification functionality built, thorough end-to-end quality assurance is required to guarantee tracking accuracy, real-time notification delivery, and platform compatibility. Inaccurate GPS data or delayed notifications directly erode customer trust and lead to missed SLA delivery windows, making full regression and functional QA essential before opening beta access.

## Success Criteria
- 100% execution of automated and manual test suites across core tracking and notification scenarios.
- Zero open critical (P1) or high (P2) defects prior to beta release gate approval.
- Real-time location polling latency verified at under 5 seconds across target mobile devices and network conditions.

## Scope

### In Scope
- End-to-end validation of operative location broadcast, ETA calculation, and customer live tracking views.
- Notification delivery verification across SMS, email, and mobile push channels.
- Dispatcher override testing and point-of-delivery confirmation workflows.
- Performance, latency, and intermittent connectivity handling validation.

### Out of Scope
- Load testing beyond 2x peak forecasted beta concurrency (deferred to General Availability QA).
- Localization and multi-language translation testing (deferred to GA roadmap).

## Acceptance Criteria
WHEN an operative updates location data, the QA test harness SHALL verify that customer tracking interfaces reflect coordinates within 5 seconds.

WHEN a field operative arrives within the geofence perimeter, the notification engine SHALL trigger the customer arrival alert within 10 seconds.

WHERE network connectivity drops during transit, the mobile client SHALL buffer location events and sync successfully upon reconnection.

IF an ETA calculation exceeds agreed SLA bounds, THEN the dispatch interface SHALL flag the variance and notify the supervising dispatcher.

## Business Rules
- Quality gate sign-off requires 100% test case execution with a minimum 95% pass rate on first run.
- Real-time tracking accuracy must be tested against both simulated movement and real-world GPS field data.
- QA test runs must validate behavior on both iOS and Android supported field devices.

## Dependencies
- Test environment populated with representative dispatch scenarios and operative telemetry data.
- SMS and Push Notification sandbox accounts configured with sufficient throughput quotas.
