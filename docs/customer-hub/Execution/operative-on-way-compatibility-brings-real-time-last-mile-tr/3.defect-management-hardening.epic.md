# Defect Management & Hardening

## Strategic Context
As testing and beta validation uncover unexpected edge cases and software defects, a structured triage and remediation cadence is critical to maintain engineering velocity and product stability. Without rigorous defect prioritization, SLA enforcement, and regression verification, unresolved bugs will disrupt pilot customer trials and jeopardize the launch timeline.

## Success Criteria
- 100% of P1 blocker defects resolved and verified within 24 hours of triage.
- 100% of P2 high-severity defects resolved and verified within 72 hours of triage.
- No regression regressions introduced across subsequent hardening patch builds.

## Scope

### In Scope
- Defect triage workflow, classification rules, and priority assignment matrix (P1/P2/P3).
- Root-cause analysis and remediation patch development for identified defects.
- Automated regression suite runs for all candidate bugfix branches.
- Weekly defect burndown tracking and stability sign-off reporting.

### Out of Scope
- Net-new feature development or architectural scope additions outside of bug fixes.
- Long-term technical debt refactoring not impacting beta stability (deferred to post-beta backlog).

## Acceptance Criteria
WHEN a new defect is logged, the triage lead SHALL classify its severity (P1/P2/P3) within 4 business hours.

WHERE a defect is classified as P1 Blocker, the engineering team SHALL deliver a verified hotfix patch within 24 hours.

IF a regression occurs during bugfix verification, THEN the test runner SHALL fail the build and notify the assigned developer immediately.

WHEN all P1 and P2 defects are verified as resolved, the QA lead SHALL issue the release hardening sign-off.

## Business Rules
- P1 defects block release deployment immediately until verified as resolved in staging.
- Any defect fix touching core location calculation logic requires dual code review and regression suite validation.
- Defect resolution status updates must be logged daily during active release hardening phases.

## Dependencies
- Defect tracking board configured with standardized severity tags and triage SLA metrics.
- CI regression test runner configured to trigger automatically on bugfix pull requests.
