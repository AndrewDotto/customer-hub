# Press Release

## Release Information

**Product/Module:** Operative On Way Compatibility
**Version:** Beta 1.0

## Headline

Operative On Way brings real-time last-mile tracking to field service, enabling seamless operative location visibility and delivery confirmation at the point of service.

## Summary

Operative On Way is a new cloud based version of OneAdvanced's existing last-mile tracking solution that extends real-time operational visibility to the final stages of field service delivery. This release achieves Beta readiness with comprehensive QA validation, enabling customer onboarding and testing to prepare for general availability market launch. The solution connects end users with field service operatives, improving visibility and customer satisfaction through dedicated communication and arrival management.

## Problem Statement

Field service organizations struggle with the final mile of service delivery—the period between operative departure and customer arrival confirmation. Customers receive no confirmation of imminent arrival, and service completion depends on manual updates. This opacity creates missed SLA windows, customer frustration, and operational inefficiency during the highest-stakes moments of service interaction.

## Solution

Operative On Way Compatibility provides real-time operative tracking and arrival notifications that close the visibility gap in the final mile. By integrating natively with Dynamic Resource Scheduler, the solution automatically broadcasts operative location status, provides customers with arrival ETAs and live tracking, from the point a job is accepted, to the time of the field service resources arrival on site.&#x20;
