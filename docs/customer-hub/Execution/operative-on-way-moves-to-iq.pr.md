# Press Release

## Headline
Operative On Way (OOW) UI Modernization: Delivering Real-Time Last-Mile Tracking on the Modern IQ Platform

## Summary
We are announcing the full production release of the modernized Operative On Way (OOW) user interface, migrating our core last-mile tracking solution from legacy MWP infrastructure to the IQ platform. This release unifies real-time field operative visibility and customer communication across web and PlatformUI Mobile standards, aligning field service capabilities with the wider OneAdvanced product ecosystem. Organizations and end users now benefit from a faster, reliable, and consistent last-mile tracking experience during service visits.

## Problem Statement
Field service organizations and remote workers rely on Operative On Way tracking to give end users accurate arrival times and operative status during last-mile visits. Legacy MWP dependencies and fragmented mobile tracking implementations kept this feature disconnected from the broader OneAdvanced platform. Aligning Operative On Way with OneAdvanced's platform standards gives every customer the same experience, built once and delivered consistently across the board.

## Solution
This release transitions the OOW UI entirely to the IQ platform, replacing legacy MWP components and standardizing mobile tracking under the PlatformUI Mobile framework. By aligning UI/UX with OneAdvanced product standards, we enable consistent, scalable delivery of last-mile tracking functionality across the organization. The modernized architecture provides robust, real-time tracking of remote workers, reliable notifications for end users, and a unified foundation that improves adoption and makes this capability available to everyone in a consistent and maintainable way.

## Customer Impact

| Key Features | Key Benefits | Value & Impact Delivered |
|---|---|---|
| IQ-Powered Last-Mile Tracking UI | Eliminates legacy MWP maintenance and delivers high-performance operative tracking aligned with OneAdvanced standards | Reduction in onboarding times, faster delivery of new field capabilities, and improved organizational adoption |
