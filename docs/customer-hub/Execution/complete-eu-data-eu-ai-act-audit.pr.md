**Product Managers must complete a compliance audit for their product(s) against the requirements of the EU Data Act and the EU AI Act**

**Release Information**

**Product/Module:** Compliance Audit Tracker **Version:** Spring Release Wave FY27

**Internal Press Release Only - Exclude from Launch Room Activity**

**Headline**

Mandatory EU Data Act and AI Act Compliance Audit Completed

**Summary**

Product Managers must complete a comprehensive compliance audit for Phase 1 products (those sold in ROI and/or sold in EU) against EU Data Act and EU AI Act requirements by December 11 2026. A dedicated audit tracker has been provided to streamline the assessment process, and any identified compliance gaps must be addressed through a remediation plan in the roadmap. This mandatory initiative ensures regulatory alignment and mitigates legal and operational risk.

**Problem Statement**

EU Data Act and AI Act regulations impose strict requirements on data handling, algorithmic transparency, and consumer rights. Product Managers lack a centralized, standardized mechanism to assess their products against these regulations, creating uncertainty about compliance status, potential exposure to regulatory penalties, and unclear remediation priorities. Without structured audit documentation, cross-functional coordination on compliance efforts becomes fragmented and difficult to track.

**Solution**

Product Operations has developed an Audit Tracker tool that guides Product Managers through a systematic compliance assessment against EU Data Act and AI Act requirements. The tracker provides a clear checklist of regulatory obligations, flagging areas of non-compliance and enabling teams to prioritize remediation efforts. Progress is monitored through Ship Room reviews with visibility into at-risk and off-track activities, ensuring accountability and transparency across the organization.

**Key Features**

1. Standardized compliance checklist aligned to EU Data Act and AI Act requirements
2. Self-assessment interface for Product Managers to evaluate product compliance status
3. Automated flagging of non-compliance gaps and remediation priorities
4. Progress tracking visible in Ship Room governance reviews

**Benefits**

1. Reduced regulatory risk and legal exposure through systematic compliance validation
2. Clearer visibility into remediation timelines and accountability for compliance gaps
3. Faster time-to-remediation by identifying and prioritizing non-compliance issues early
4. Consistent compliance standards across all products and product lines
5. Structured governance oversight that connects compliance efforts to business outcomes
6. Streamlined cross-functional coordination between Product, Legal/Compliance, and Product Operations

**Customer Quote (Imagined)**

"The Audit Tracker gave us a clear roadmap for EU compliance and eliminated the guesswork. We identified three critical gaps in data handling practices that we've now prioritized for the Spring wave. It's exactly the structure we needed to move from uncertain to confident about our regulatory posture."

**Call to Action**

All Product Managers must access the Audit Tracker immediately and complete the compliance assessment for Phase 2 products by December 11 2026. Product Managers must create a remediation plan in their roadmap with agreed completion date with Madalina Cristea. Complete the EU Data Act and AI Act Compliance Audit Tracker by the deadline.
